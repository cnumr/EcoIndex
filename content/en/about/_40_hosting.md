## Hosting by Hodi

{{% /info_2_columns_col1 %}}

{{% info_2_columns_col2 %}}

This site is hosted by Hodi, a French hosting provider whose infrastructure in France limits its environmental footprint on two levels: the eco-responsible datacenters that house it, and the way the host optimizes the use of its servers.

These datacenters prioritize refurbished hardware (from reuse rather than new production), renewable energy supply, and a PUE target below 1.3 at full capacity.

On the operations side, half of the CPUs are automatically shut down at night when load allows, which reduces electricity consumption, and malicious bot traffic — nearly 30% of global web traffic — is blocked by default: fewer useless requests and less energy wasted.

Hodi goes further by integrating EcoIndex measurement by default on its customers' sites. Because a site's footprint does not depend only on the host: how it is designed and used matters just as much. The goal is to help everyone measure, understand, and reduce their own footprint.

Beyond its infrastructure, Hodi is committed to digital development in Africa, and as much as possible to responsible digital practices on the continent. Its servers in France directly serve this ambition: they offer African companies expanding in Europe quality hosting, complementary to their infrastructure in Africa.

Do not hesitate to [contact](https://hodi.host/en/contact/) them for your hosting needs.
